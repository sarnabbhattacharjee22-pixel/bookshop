About NovaBooks

Connecting readers in Bangladesh with the world's best stories, ideas, and knowledge.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, NovaBooks started with a simple yet powerful idea: to make reading accessible, affordable, and enjoyable for everyone in Bangladesh. We noticed how difficult it was for readers to find a reliable online bookstore that offered both local literature and international bestsellers with seamless delivery.

"A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The man who never reads lives only one."

What began as a small inventory operating out of a single room in Dhaka has now grown into one of the most trusted e-commerce platforms for book lovers nationwide. We are deeply committed to fostering a reading culture by offering an extensive catalog that ranges from fiction and academic textbooks to self-development and children's books.

Our journey is driven by our passion for books and our dedication to customer satisfaction. We don't just sell books; we deliver stories, knowledge, and inspiration right to your doorstep.
